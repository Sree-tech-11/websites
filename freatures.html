Features

Bestcon's AAC (Autoclaved Aerated Concrete) blocks are considered "eco-green" due to their environmentally friendly features like being made from recycled materials (like fly-ash), having a low carbon footprint during production, excellent thermal insulation properties which reduce energy consumption, and being lightweight and easy to transport, minimizing construction waste and transportation emissions; making them a sustainable building material choice.

Key eco-friendly benefits of AAC blocks:

  • Recycled materials: AAC blocks often utilize industrial byproducts like fly-ash, reducing the need for virgin materials.
  • Low energy production: The manufacturing process of AAC blocks requires less energy compared to traditional brick making.
  • Excellent thermal insulation: AAC blocks provide superior thermal insulation, which helps maintain comfortable indoor temperatures and reduces heating/cooling energy needs.
  • Lightweight: Their lightweight nature reduces the need for heavy construction machinery and minimizes transportation emissions.
  • Minimal waste: AAC blocks can be precisely cut to size, minimizing waste during construction.
  • Non-toxic materials: AAC blocks are made from non-toxic components, making them safe for occupants.
