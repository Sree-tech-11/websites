Joint mortor

Bestcon's Jointing Mortar is an (AAC) blocks jointing adhesivethat gives excellent bonding between two blocks. It is also suitable for the bonding of most of all construction materials in the interior and exterior. It is specially designed to provide higher bonding as a result of higher tensile adhesion strength. Designed for use with water to produce high strength thixotropic mortar, for laying most of all Aerated Lightweight concrete AAC Blocks, Fly ash bricks.

    Properties

  • Base : Cement with modified polymer powder
  • Density: 2.0 g/cm3
  • Water Demand: 20 – 21%
  • Tensile adhesion Strength: 1.0 N/mm²
  • Self-Curing: Yes
