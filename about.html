Welcome To Bestcon

At BESTCON, we are dedicated to revolutionizing the construction industry with our high-quality AAC (Autoclaved Aerated Concrete) blocks. Founded in 2023, we are the largest producer of AAC blocks in South Tamilnadu. We have a single plant installed capacity of 345 m3 per day which has production capability of manufacturing 24000 blocks per day. The plant has an expansion capability of 690 m3 per day.
At BESTCON We have committed ourselves in establishing ourselves as a leader in the manufacturing of innovative building materials that prioritize reliability, sustainability, efficiency, and durability.

Aspiration and Purpose

Vision

At BESTCON, Our vision is to build a brighter, more sustainable future through innovative construction solutions. We believe in the power of AAC blocks to not only improve construction processes but also to enhance the overall quality of built environments. By using eco-friendly / recycled materials in our optimized manufacturing process, we put ourselves stringently to aim to reduce the carbon footprint of buildings and contributing to a greener planet.

Mission

Our mission is to deliver state-of-the-art AAC blocks that meet the diverse needs of architects, builders, and developers. We strive to uphold the highest standards of quality, safety, and environmental responsibility in our production processes. Through continuous research and development, we seek to advance our technology, ensuring that we remain at the forefront of the AAC industry.
